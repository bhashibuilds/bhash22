DevOps Cost Intelligence Platform

Real-time cost allocation and waste detection

exploring

Show engineering teams exactly what their features cost to run. Real-time cost allocation across multi-cloud environments, automated waste detection, and recommendations for optimization.

Why it matters: Most teams have no idea what they're actually spending on. They ship features without understanding the infrastructure cost burden.

Target: B2B SaaS for mid-market to enterprise eng teams

Tags: FinOps, DevOps Tools, Cost Management, Multi-cloud

On-Call Optimization SaaS

Intelligent scheduling that learns team patterns

idea

On-call burnout is real. This learns team patterns, predicts high-incident times, and suggests optimal rotations. Smart handoffs, better alert routing, and burnout prevention.

Why it matters: Current tools are dumb – they just rotate people on a schedule. This gets smarter with time.

Target: B2B SaaS for SRE teams

Tags: SRE Tools, Team Productivity, On-call Management

Automated Runbook Generator

AI-powered incident playbooks

idea

Generate incident runbooks from your existing metrics, logs, and historical incidents. AI learns your environment's failure modes and suggests fix sequences.

Why it matters: Writing runbooks is tedious. Most teams don't have them. AI can generate context-aware, personalized runbooks.

Target: B2B SaaS for observability platforms

Tags: AI/ML, Observability, Incident Response, Automation

Incident Postmortem Assistant

Automated synthesis & action items

exploring
